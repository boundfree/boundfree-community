Third-party components bundled in this distribution:

    * pocketfft (hdronly variant) (BSD-3-Clause) — used by the DSP FFT path.

  Python sidecar (frozen):

sidecar. They are bundled as a consequence of collecting MNE and are NOT
directly invoked by the sidecar's format readers; their licenses are included
for completeness.

This distribution does NOT bundle PySide6, pyqtgraph, or cryptography. Those
components belong to the legacy Python/PySide6 reference build and are not
part of this native release.
